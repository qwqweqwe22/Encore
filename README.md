<h1 align="center">Encore</h1>
<p align="center">A music player for the songs on your own device — Windows and Android.</p>

## Features
- Plays the music files on your PC or phone (MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, OGG, AIFF…)
- Playlists, smart shuffle (spreads each artist out) and reshuffle
- Volume boost up to 400%, 10-band EQ, auto volume leveling, crossfade, sleep timer
- Stats: top artists, songs and albums, listening habits
- Visualizer with 8 styles and lots of customization
- Edit song info and cover art
- Last.fm scrobbling
- Updates itself — when a new version comes out, the app offers it

## Install

### Windows
1. Download **Encore-Setup-x.y.z.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/qwqweqwe22/Encore/releases/latest).
2. Run it. Windows may show *“Windows protected your PC”* because the app isn't from a big company — click **More info → Run anyway**.

### Android (8.0 or newer)
1. Download **Encore-x.y.z.apk** from the [latest release](https://github.com/qwqweqwe22/Encore/releases/latest) on your phone.
2. Open it. Android asks you to allow installing apps from your browser or files app — allow it, then tap **Install**.
3. When Encore opens, turn on **All files access** so it can find your music.

## Updates
Encore checks this page for new versions. When one is available you'll see **Update ready** in the app — tap it and the update installs. Every update is signed, and Encore only installs updates signed by this project.

## Privacy
Your music, playlists and stats stay on your device. Encore only goes online to check for updates, look up artist photos (just the artist's name is sent to Deezer), and — only if you connect it — scrobble to Last.fm.

This repository only holds the app's downloads.
